Fixed a blank, empty window on computers with NVIDIA's proprietary graphics driver (reported from Bazzite with KDE on Wayland). When that driver is loaded, GrepFocus now switches off WebKit's DMA-BUF renderer at startup, the known workaround for this driver; other graphics cards are unaffected. Set WEBKIT_DISABLE_DMABUF_RENDERER=0 in your environment to force the default back. The README's Known limits lists further steps for other setups that show a blank window.
